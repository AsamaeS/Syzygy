# Hardware Glossary

**ADC** – Analog-to-Digital Converter  
**BOM** – Bill of Materials  
**ESP32** – Espressif microcontroller with Wi-Fi  
**GPIO** – General Purpose Input/Output  
**I2C** – Inter-Integrated Circuit (two-wire bus)  
**INA226** – Digital current/voltage/power monitor IC  
**MQTT** – Lightweight messaging protocol used for telemetry  
**Reference Node** – The single microgrid node designed as the base architecture  
**Shunt Resistor** – Low-value resistor used to measure current  
**TVS** – Transient Voltage Suppressor  
**Wokwi** – Browser-based ESP32 simulator
